The Hotel Positioning method

Positioning is the reason guests choose you.

One sentence, backed by facts, that sets you apart from the hotel three hundred metres away. The method finds it in six steps.

Request the free positioning read →

Reading time: about 3 minutes. One example for every step.

Why it matters

If guests can't tell you apart, they compare prices.

Search your area on Booking: fifty rectangles. Same photos, same stars, same adjectives.

If hotels look alike, guests are left with one criterion: price. Positioning gives them another one.

The method

Six steps. One hotel, from start to finish.

Each step rules something out. At the end, one sentence is left.

ExampleA 70-room business hotel near the trade fair grounds, in a city in northern Italy. Four competitors within a kilometre.

  1. Step 01

    The real need

    What does the guest want from you, beyond the room?

    In the example

    People coming for the fair aren't looking for a bed. They want to work until their last Friday appointment without dragging a suitcase around.

  2. Step 02

    The alternatives

    What do they compare you with, even beyond hotels?

    In the example

    Not just the four hotels by the fair. Also the Thursday evening train home, to avoid another night away.

  3. Step 03

    What sets you apart

    What facts do you already have, written in your reviews?

    In the example

    The same sentence keeps coming up in the reviews: “they let me keep the room until six”. The hotel had done it for years without writing it down anywhere.

  4. Step 04

    The right guest

    Who are you the right choice for? And who do you leave to others?

    In the example

    The professional who comes back for every fair, for two or three nights. Weekend groups are left to the other hotels.

  5. Step 05

    The category

    Which category can you become the reference for?

    In the example

    Not “4-star hotel near the fair”, like the other four. The hotel for people who work until Friday afternoon: in that category, it's the only one.

  6. Step 06

    The timing

    Is there a trend moving in your direction?

    In the example

    The city's fairs are going from three days to four and closing on Friday afternoon. The need grows every year.

The sentence that remains The business hotel with 6 pm check-out on Fridays.

If your last meeting is in the afternoon, there's nothing else like it. For your hotel the sentence will be different. The positioning read tells you whether today's line is replaceable and where to look for your reason.

A real case

Silva Splendid, Fiuggi. Same hotel, a different sentence.

118 rooms and the largest SPA in Lazio, 1,600 m². Yet it presented itself like plenty of others.

Before 4-star hotel with SPA.

A list of services. It spoke to everyone, which means to no one.

→
After The Wellness Hotel of Fiuggi.

A fact instead of an adjective. The word “The” says there is only one. Families with children are almost all left out.

What the case doesn't prove. Positioning and renovation went together. The first set the direction, the owners' investment made it credible.

Questions

Three frequent questions.

Do I need to rebuild my website?

No. You start from the most visited page. Rewriting everything before deciding the position repeats the same mistake with new words.

Do I need a renovation?

No. Silva built a new fact. Veridia, in Chia, already had one and wasn't using it: today it is The nature resort of Chia. The second case is the more common one.

Does it work for a chain?

The method is designed for independent hotels, where decisions are made in-house.
