The 200 attributes

Not a chapter to read. A toolbox to open when you need it. Scroll through. For each one, mark what you have, what you could have, what you will never be. Then choose your handful.

Three rules

  1. 1

    Choose a few. A hotel that claims thirty claims none. Two or three that fit together are worth more than thirty unconnected ones.

  2. 2

    State them. If the guest cannot find an attribute written down, it does not exist for the market.

  3. 3

    Keep them. One stated and then broken is worse than silence: it becomes the one-star review.

The 200 attributes

Your handful

No stars yet. Go back to the list and choose the few you want to build on.

Three checking questions

  1. 1. Do they fit together? Do they describe the same hotel?
  2. 2. Are they things the guest does not find claimed by others in the area?
  3. 3. Can you defend them every day, without ever breaking them?
