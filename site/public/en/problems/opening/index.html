N°01

A well-made product, on its own, doesn't explain why to choose it.

The day before I opened my first restaurant, I was standing in the middle of the dining room. Everything was ready.

I had even chosen the plates myself. Not the menu: the ceramics.

Ten minutes of satisfaction. Then a question: if a customer asks me tomorrow, “Why should I come to you?”, what do I say?

I had invested everything in how the place looked. Nothing in what it was.

The first year, it ran on curiosity. In the second, the curiosity ran out. I paid dearly for it.

A hotel doesn't get into trouble because of the wrong door handles. It gets into trouble when it starts out the same as the others. And a hotel that starts out the same has only one lever: price, from day one.

Your renders look like everyone else's.

05

The concept is “boutique hotel”.

Twenty years ago it was a position. Today it's a category, and a crowded one.

Saying “boutique hotel” today is almost the same as saying “hotel”.

06

The project describes the place, not the hotel.

The sea, the lake, the hills.

That way, you promote every hotel in the area too.

07

09

Being specific means choosing. And choosing means leaving some guests out.

First the compass. Then the walls.

Case · Silva Splendid · Fiuggi

From “4-star hotel with spa” to The Wellness Hotel of Fiuggi.

  1. 118 rooms and the largest spa in Lazio. Yet it spoke to everyone, and nobody remembered it.
  2. The choice: a fact instead of an adjective, a category with the definite article (in Italian, L’Hotel Benessere di Fiuggi), and almost no families any more.
  3. In five years, revenue goes from €5.6 million to €7 million. The average rate roughly doubles.
Annual revenue €5.6M €7M

Positioning and renovation together: the first set the direction, the owners' investment made it credible. In that order.

FAQ

I don't have a website yet. Does it make sense to start now?

Yes, it's the best time. Before the foundations, you decide where to build. The same goes for identity. Without a homepage the teardown doesn't apply: write to me at consulting@francescoastolfi.net and we'll start from the full analysis.

The architect has already defined the concept.

The architect decides how your hotel looks. Positioning decides why guests choose it. If the reason comes first, the project takes it into account. If it comes later, the project gets redone.

Isn't it too early?

No. It's too late the day after opening: from then on, every correction costs three times as much.

Does it work for a smaller hotel too?

Yes, often even more so: a 40-room hotel can choose a precise niche; a 200-room hotel has to hold several segments together.
