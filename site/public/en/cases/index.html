Case 01 · Silva Splendid · Fiuggi · 118 rooms

It spoke to everyone. Now it’s the wellness hotel.

1 · Where it started 4-star hotel with SPA in Fiuggi.

The historic hotel in Fiuggi, the most reviewed in town. Older guests in summer, young couples in winter, then families, children, pets.

It spoke to everyone, so nobody remembered it for anything.

2 · The choice The Wellness Hotel of Fiuggi.

With “The”: not “a” wellness hotel, the reference. The proof is a fact: the largest SPA in Lazio, 1,600 m², more than a hundred treatments, a doctor.

And almost out of the family segment. The trade-off that makes the rest credible.

3 · The numbers
€7Mannual revenue, up from €5.6M, in five years
×2average rate, roughly
9on Booking, up from 8.6
1,600m² of SPA, the largest in Lazio

4 · What it proves

  • That you can raise the rate, lose the lower-end guests and see your reviews go up.
  • That even the biggest, most reviewed hotel in the area blends in if it speaks to everyone.

5 · What it doesn’t prove

  • That positioning alone doubles revenue. There was also a renovation, paid for by the owners.
  • The result comes from both together: positioning set the direction, the investment made it credible.
02

Case 02 · Veridia Resort · Chia · Sardinia

A hundred resorts by the sea. Only one in nature.

1 · Where it started Beach, pool, relaxation.

The same line as a hundred Sardinian resorts. Sold as a package by tour operators, like any family holiday village.

Other people set the price.

2 · The choice The nature resort of Chia.

The place, not the sea. Nothing needed building: the fact was already there, it just had no name.

An international soft brand brings in European guests and leaves the resort independent.

3 · The numbers
>60%of sales are direct
n/abefore-and-after revenue: the repositioning is recent

4 · What it proves

  • That the distinctive fact is often already there and nobody uses it. Here it was the place.
  • That you can have international distribution without leaving the price to tour operators.

5 · What it doesn’t prove

  • A rise in revenue: for now there is no comparison to show.
  • The evidence is something else: the resort has taken back control of its name, category, guest and channels.

Case 03 · Yume Ramen · 5 restaurants

An entrepreneur’s case. Not a consultant’s.

Yume Ramen is my own company, started with my own capital. It isn’t a consulting job, and I don’t present it as one.

1 · Where it started Nothing: no venue, no name.

Zero customers, zero revenue. The capital at risk was mine.

2 · The choice Yume, Japanese for dream.

Not a place where you eat ramen: a place where you stop and think about your own dream.

Decided before the first kitchen opened.

3 · The numbers
€2.5Min revenue, starting from zero
5Yume Ramen restaurants
+5Enzu Asian Food restaurants, from a ghost kitchen launched in 2020

4 · What it proves

  • That the position is decided before investing, not after.
  • That I know from the inside the decisions of people who risk their own capital.

5 · What it doesn’t prove

  • That it works for a hotel: it’s restaurants. The method is the same, the market isn’t.
  • Consulting work: here the client was me.
  • That it always works out. I’ve invested my own capital several times, and it hasn’t always gone well.
