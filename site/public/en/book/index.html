An example from the book

Open Booking and search your area. Find your hotel.

The book starts here: look at the results as a guest who doesn't know you.

Fifty rectangles. One is your hotel. Finding it isn't easy.

The same pool seen from a drone. The same four stars. The same adjectives:

WelcomingComfortableSurrounded by greeneryJust steps from the sea

Guests don't read, they scroll. If the hotels all look the same, they are left with one criterion: price.

Traffic can be bought. The reason to be chosen has to be built.

The method

Six steps. In the end they fit on one sheet.

The book follows them in the order they need to be done. Skipping one is why many repositioning projects stall in the meeting room.

  1. 1

    The real need

    What the guest asks of your hotel.

  2. 2

    The alternatives

    The ones they really have, even if they aren't hotels.

  3. 3

    What makes you unique

    What you already have, read in your reviews. Facts, not adjectives.

  4. 4

    The right person

    Who values that, and exists in large enough numbers.

  5. 5

    The category

    The one you can become the reference point for.

  6. 6

    The trend

    The one to follow, when there is one.
