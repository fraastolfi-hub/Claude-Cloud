    The algorithm

    21 checks, four areas, one verdict.

    I built it on the criteria of the positioning analyses I do by hand. Each area weighs differently: the message counts more than speed.

    1 · Weighs most

    Message

    • Does the first screen give guests an answer, or just “Welcome” and a photo?
    • Is it clear who the hotel is for, and why to choose it?
    • The substitution test: put a competitor's name in place of yours. Is the text still true?
    • How many stock phrases: “in the heart of”, “warm welcome”, “all the comforts”.

    I show you the sentence and the proof. I don't rewrite it: that's the analysis's job.

    2

    Proof and conversion

    • Reviews and portal scores, visible on the page.
    • The booking button on the first mobile screen.
    • Why booking with you beats booking on Booking.
    • Tappable phone number, WhatsApp, FAQs, awards.

    Each point comes with a one-line instruction: you can fix it yourself.

    3

    Speed

    • The mobile score, from 0 to 100.
    • How many seconds pass before you see anything.
    • Whether the page jumps while it loads.
    • How much the photos weigh.

    Measured with Google PageSpeed. You pass the instructions on to your web agency.

    4

    How AI reads you

    • Asked “why choose this hotel?”, what ChatGPT would answer after reading your page.
    • Which benefits it understands, and what it doesn't.
    • Structured data and description for search engines and AI.

    Guests and agencies are starting to ask AI where to stay. It answers with what it finds written.

    What it doesn't do

    It's not a redesign. It's not a thirty-line scorecard.

    One verdict, three traffic lights and the three to five points that matter. It takes two minutes to read.

    Speed and proof can be fixed with a practical instruction. The message can't: if it comes out amber or red, the reason a guest should choose your hotel has to be found, not written better. It starts from your guests' reviews.

    That's why the report ends with the ways to fix things: your agency for the technical points; for the message, the positioning analysis if you want me to do it, delivered in English, or the book (Italian edition) if you want the method. And if you'd like to talk it through with me, just reply to the report email.

    What happens after the check-up?

    Send the report to your agency: speed and proof can be fixed with the one-line instructions. If the problem is the message, talk to me: reply to the report email, I read it myself.

    How do you decide whether the page deserves a budget?

    Red message: not yet, because the campaign would bring people to a page that doesn't say why to choose you. Green message and nothing red elsewhere: yes. Everything else: almost, with the changes to make first.

    How much does it cost?

    Nothing. You leave the page address and your email, and the report arrives there.

    Why on mobile?

    People who arrive from an ad on Instagram or Google are usually on their phone. The first screen they see decides whether they stay.

    How does the algorithm work?

    I turned the criteria I use in positioning analyses into 21 checks, with my own rules and thresholds. Speed is measured by Google PageSpeed Insights. Message, proof and the AI reading are assessed by an artificial intelligence on the public text of the page, following those rules. The AI receives only the text of the page, never your email.

    Can I check a landing page instead of the homepage?

    Yes. In fact, if you are about to launch a campaign, check the very page the ad will lead to.

    Does it work with every website?

    Almost. If the page is built entirely with JavaScript, sometimes I can't read it. In that case I tell you straight away and look at it myself.
