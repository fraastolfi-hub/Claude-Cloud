    N°01

    Three possible profiles. Each one points to a job to be done.

    Even the best profile has to be maintained.

    0-5 points

    Invisible

    On Booking your hotel is one of fifty rectangles. Guests can't tell it apart from the others, so they decide on price.

    6-10 points

    Replaceable

    Your hotel has something true about it. Guests feel it, but can't put it into words. So a cheaper hotel nearby is all it takes.

    11-14 points

    Positioned

    A precise reason to be chosen, in one sentence guests recognise as true. It's rare, and it has to be maintained.

    N°02

    Where the questions come from.

    Each question is a sign I look for when I analyse a hotel for the first time: the thirty-second sentence, the covered logo, interchangeable reviews, the price that drops in September, a message written by the people who run the hotel rather than by its guests.

    What this test doesn't do: it doesn't analyse your hotel, it measures your answers. It tells you where to look, not what to write.

    For that you need the method applied to your hotel, with your reviews and your competitors.
