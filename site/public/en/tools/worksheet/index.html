The 6 Steps of Positioning

You don’t read it. You fill it in. One afternoon, some honesty, and the will to cross things out until it holds. You start from the guest. Never from the product.

1
Step 1

The guest: the problem or the deep desire

Nobody wants a holiday. They want to go home having slept properly for the first time in months. Write the emotion, not the service.

You need an answer to move on.

2
Step 2

The alternatives: who you are up against

Group them by approach, not by stars.

3
Step 3

What makes you unique: a fact, not an idea

What is the one true thing you have that none of those names has? Verifiable in two seconds. Then: so what? Why should the guest care?

That is what everyone says. Look for the fact a competitor cannot claim without lying.

You need an answer to move on.

4
Step 4

The target: who cannot do without you

A few people who love it are worth more than many who are vaguely interested.

5
Step 5

The category: the box you occupy in the guest’s mind

In one sentence: “the hotel of / for ___”. The box that opens when they think of you.

That way you have chosen nothing. You need a box that nobody in your area holds.

6
Step 6

The trend: the wave (if there is one)

Two ruthless tests

Before you fall in love with your idea.

The opposite test

Could a competitor say the exact opposite without sounding foolish? If not, it is a platitude in disguise.
