Who it’s for

Let’s be clear. It isn’t for everyone.

It’s for you if

  • You run an independent 4 or 5-star hotel or resort.
  • You want a rate that holds without discounts, even off season.
  • You’re ready to change words, offers and priorities, if needed.

It isn’t for you if

  • You think the only problem is Booking, and the fix is a lower rate on your website.
  • You hope a new website or a renovation will be enough on their own.
  • You want more followers, not more direct bookings.

I’m telling you upfront, so neither of us wastes time.
