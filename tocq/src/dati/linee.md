# Tocq Hotel Milano: transport and events (research of 26 Oct 2026)

Starting points: the **Garibaldi FS** metro (M2, M5) is a 3-minute walk. **Milano Porta Garibaldi** station is a 5-minute walk; it has surface platforms for regional trains, the S11, the S7/S8 and the Malpensa Express, plus underground Passante platforms for S1/S2/S5/S6/S12/S13.

### Notes and uncertain points
- **Rho Fiera**: Fiera Milano's site says the S11 stops at Rho Fiera only on weekdays. The S11 has run Rho to Como San Giovanni since Dec 2025 (Chiasso is now served by TILO S10). Some high-speed trains stop at Rho Fiera only during the big fairs; check each one.
- **MiCo gates**: South Wing gates 1, 2, 3, 4, 11, 12 and 16 are on Scarampo/Eginardo/Colleoni. North Wing gates 5-10 are on Scarampo/Teodorico. Gate 16 = Piazzale Carlo Magno. Portello serves all of them.
- **Monza GP**: In 2026 there were 21 extra non-stop trains P. Garibaldi to Biassono on the Sunday, a "Treno+Navetta Nera" ticket at €10 return, and a Trenord Day Pass at €13. The 2027 offers aren't out yet. I didn't read monzanet.it directly, and I haven't verified Villasanta as an access point.
- **Malpensa**: Departures from Garibaldi are about 10 minutes after Centrale (:25/:55), so roughly :35/:05. This comes from sample timetables; check it.
- **Linate**: S5/S6 to Forlanini + M4 for 2 stops is a possible alternative; I haven't verified it.
- **Orio**: The Bergamo to Orio rail link is still under construction. RFI targeted end of 2026, the ministry (MIT) says by 2027, and no opening is confirmed.
- **M2**: It has periodic summer closures between Cadorna and Garibaldi (one ran 19/7-7/9 in 2025). Check ATM.
