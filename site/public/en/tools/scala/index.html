From fact to transformation

What makes you unique does not sell on its own. You have to move it up one rung at a time, to the point where the guest is moved. The fact gives credibility. The benefit makes it concrete. But the transformation is what sells, because people buy to become a better version of themselves.

Transformationsells

“You get home on Sunday evening and, for the first time in months, you feel truly rested, and the people you live with notice.”

Benefitconcreteness

“You sleep with the window open and nothing wakes you.”

Factcredibility

“Not a single car passes within two kilometres.”

And now the button

The rule applies to the CTA too. On the button you don’t put the fact: a room, a date, a form. You put the piece of transformation the guest is buying.

“Find out more”→“See the room that looks onto the woods”
“Contact us”→“Find out what it costs to switch off for three days”
“Book now”→“Book your nights of silence”
“Request a quote”→“Get your tailor-made programme”

Your homepage, your headlines and your emails come from these three lines. The fact for credibility, the transformation to get the booking.
