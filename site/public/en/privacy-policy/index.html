This policy describes how personal data collected through the website hotelpositioning.com is processed, pursuant to Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Legislative Decree 196/2003, as amended by Legislative Decree 101/2018 (Italian Privacy Code).

01Data controller

The data controller is Francesco Astolfi, sole proprietorship with registered office at Via Cupa 5, 47923 Rimini (RN), Italy, VAT no. 04283940403 (“Hotel Positioning”). For any matter relating to personal data, you can write to consulting@francescoastolfi.net.

02What data we collect

We collect only the data that the data subject voluntarily enters in the forms on the website, plus the technical data needed for the pages to work.

Request for the positioning read: name and role, name of the hotel, website address, email, number of rooms, the 2-3 competitors indicated, an optional line on what guests should understand about the hotel, and the page from which the form was submitted.

Positioning test: email, and the profile and score resulting from the test.

The book's tools (pages /bonus/ and /cassetta/; in English, /en/tools/): name, email, type of property; on the /cassetta/ page also the number of rooms (in bands) and the optional choice to receive follow-up emails.

Guide “Che hotel sei?” (“What kind of hotel are you?”) (page /guida/): name, email, number of rooms (in bands) and the optional choice to receive follow-up emails. The number of rooms is used to suggest the next step suited to the property.

Positioning analysis questionnaire (for those who have purchased the analysis): name of the contact person, email, details of the property (name, location, type, rooms, seasonality, average price, website, booking channels and their shares), the answers to the questions on positioning and public links to reviews. The answers are saved in the data subject's browser until they submit them.

For each form we also record the date of submission and the consent given. We do not request or process special categories of data (Art. 9 GDPR).

Browsing data. In the course of their normal operation, the systems hosting the website acquire certain technical data such as the IP address, the type of browser and the pages requested. This data is used to deliver the website, to keep it secure and to filter automated submissions (spam); it is not used to identify users or for profiling.

Data collected through statistics and marketing cookies, only with the data subject's consent: browser or device identifiers, pages visited, source of the visit, actions taken on the website (for example, submitting a form). Details are in the section “Cookies and browser storage”.

03Purposes and legal basis

Data is processed for the following purposes:

a) Responding to and assessing requests: examining the property indicated, checking that it meets the requirements for the positioning read (4 and 5-star hotels and resorts), preparing the positioning read or the analysis requested, and contacting the data subject again. Legal basis: performance of pre-contractual measures taken at the data subject's request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

b) Sending the content requested: the test result, the book's tools and the Tagliando Hotel report (/tagliando/; in English, /en/tagliando/), which automatically reads the public page indicated. For Tagliando Hotel we keep the page address, the name of the property, the email and the outcome of the traffic lights. Legal basis: performance of the data subject's request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

c) Sending communications about Hotel Positioning (content on positioning, the book, new tools, initiatives) to those who have requested them by ticking the relevant box in the form for the book's tools. On the /cassetta/ and /guida/ pages this box is separate from the one needed to receive the tools and is optional: without it, only the tools are sent. Those who tick it receive, over the following two weeks, a few emails on how to use the tools and on the book Hotel Positioning, then one communication about every two weeks; the link “Il libro ce l’hai già?” (“Do you already have the book?”) in those emails stops the messages dedicated to the book. Those who take the positioning test receive only the result, unless they give further consent. In Tagliando Hotel the box for follow-up emails is separate and optional: without it, only the report is sent. Legal basis: the data subject's consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which can be withdrawn at any time via the link in every email.

d) Publishing an analysis as a case study, with the name of the property or in anonymous form, only if the property has authorised it in writing. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

e) Website security and abuse prevention, including the spam filter on forms. Legal basis: the controller's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

f) Complying with legal obligations, for example tax and accounting obligations when the request leads to a contractual relationship. Legal basis: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

g) Statistics on website use with Google Analytics: visits, most-read pages, traffic sources, in aggregate form. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given through the cookie banner.

h) Measuring and optimising advertising campaigns on Facebook and Instagram with the Meta Pixel, including creating audiences in order to show ads to people who have visited the website. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given through the cookie banner.

i) Meta Conversions API: when someone who has accepted marketing cookies submits the form on the /cassetta/ or /guida/ pages, the website's server notifies Meta of the sign-up (“Lead” event), in order to measure campaign results and avoid counting the same sign-up recorded by the Pixel twice. The following are transmitted: the email address and name in encrypted form (SHA-256 hash, not readable), the IP address, the browser type, the identifiers of the _fbp and _fbc cookies and an event code. Without consent to marketing cookies, this data is not sent. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given through the cookie banner.

04Whether providing data is mandatory

Providing the data marked as mandatory in the forms is necessary in order to respond to the request: without it, it is not possible to assess the request, prepare the positioning read or send the content requested. Fields marked as optional may be left blank.

05How data is processed

Data is processed using IT tools and with technical and organisational measures appropriate to prevent its loss, unlawful use and unauthorised access. Positioning reads and analyses are prepared personally by the controller. Support tools, including artificial intelligence tools for translation, may be used to examine public information about the property (website, reviews, online listings); personal data entered in the forms is not used to train artificial intelligence models. We do not use automated decision-making processes that produce legal effects on the data subject (Art. 22 GDPR).

06Recipients of the data

Data is not sold or transferred to third parties for their own purposes. It may be processed on behalf of the controller, as data processors (Art. 28 GDPR), by the suppliers that make it possible to provide the service:

Netlify, Inc. (United States): website hosting, receipt of forms, spam filtering and running the function that forwards data to Brevo.

Anthropic, PBC (United States): only for Tagliando Hotel, automatic reading of the public text of the page indicated. It receives the address and text of the page and, if entered, the name of the property; it does not receive the email or any other contact details.

Google (PageSpeed Insights): only for Tagliando Hotel, measuring the speed of the page indicated. It receives only the page address.

Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France): contact management, sending emails to contacts and internal notifications to the controller. Data is processed on servers in the European Union.

Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC: only with consent, Google Tag Manager, the tool that loads statistics and marketing tools on the website according to the choice made in the banner, and Google Analytics 4 for statistics, with the IP address not stored.

Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd: only with consent, the Meta Pixel and Conversions API for measuring campaigns on Facebook and Instagram. For the collection and transmission of data through the Pixel and the Conversions API, the controller and Meta are joint controllers (Art. 26 GDPR), under the joint controllership agreement drawn up by Meta; subsequent processing is carried out by Meta as an independent controller, in accordance with its own privacy policy.

Data may also be disclosed to tax and legal advisers bound by confidentiality, and to the authorities where required by law. Payment for the analysis, when requested, takes place on the Stripe platform, and purchase of the book on Amazon: in these cases the data is processed by those parties as independent controllers, in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

07Transfers outside the European Union

Some suppliers (Netlify, Google, Meta, Anthropic) are based in the United States or may process data in the United States. The transfer takes place on the basis of the European Commission's adequacy decision on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for suppliers that participate in it, or of the standard contractual clauses approved by the Commission (Art. 46 GDPR).

08Retention period

Positioning read requests: up to 24 months from the last contact. If the request leads to an engagement, the necessary data is kept for the duration of the relationship and, for tax obligations, for 10 years.

Contacts subscribed to communications: until consent is withdrawn or erasure is requested. If there is no interaction for 24 months, the contact may be removed.

Consent to publication of the analysis: until withdrawn; after withdrawal, the case is no longer used in new publications.

Google Analytics statistics: 14 months, then automatically deleted.

Meta Pixel data: according to the periods indicated in the section “Cookies and browser storage” and in Meta's privacy policy.

Browsing data and technical logs: for the time strictly necessary, according to the settings of the hosting providers.

09Cookies and browser storage

On the first visit, a banner asks for consent to non-essential cookies. Until a choice is made, and if consent is refused, no statistics or marketing tool is loaded. Refusing is as easy as accepting: just use the “Reject” button or the X on the banner. The choice is valid for 6 months and can be changed at any time with the “Cookie preferences” link at the bottom of every page.

Technical, always active: the browser's local storage (localStorage) keeps the cookie choice (hp-consent, 6 months), the fact that a notice has already been closed, the choices made in the positioning library, access to the book's tools (library_unlocked) and the answers entered in the tools; session storage keeps, for a few seconds, the code of the sign-up just submitted, which prevents it from being recorded twice. This data stays on the user's device and is not sent to the controller. It does not require consent.

Google Tag Manager, only with consent: it is loaded only after consent to statistics or marketing cookies and passes the choice made on to the tools (Google Consent Mode): each tool starts only if its category has been accepted. Google Tag Manager does not set cookies of its own.

Statistics, only with consent: Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Ltd) sets the _ga and _ga_* cookies, lasting up to 2 years, to distinguish visits and measure use of the website in aggregate form.

Marketing, only with consent: the Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd) sets the _fbp cookie (up to 3 months) and, when arriving from an ad, _fbc (up to 3 months), to measure campaign results and show ads to people who have visited the website.

Cookies can also be deleted from the browser settings. Refusing statistics and marketing cookies does not limit use of the website.

10Rights of the data subject

The data subject has the right to access their data, to request its rectification or erasure, to obtain restriction of processing, to object to processing based on legitimate interest and to receive the data in a structured format (portability), pursuant to Articles 15-22 GDPR. They may withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. Every email sent contains a link to unsubscribe with one click.

11How to exercise your rights

Rights can be exercised by writing to consulting@francescoastolfi.net. The controller responds without undue delay and in any case within one month of the request, which may be extended in the cases provided for by Art. 12 GDPR.

12Complaints to the supervisory authority

A data subject who considers that the processing of their data infringes the regulations has the right to lodge a complaint with the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, the Italian Data Protection Authority (www.garanteprivacy.it), or with the supervisory authority of the Member State in which they live or work or in which the alleged infringement took place.

13Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated over time, for example when services or suppliers change. The version in force is always the one published on this page, with the date of the last update shown at the top.
