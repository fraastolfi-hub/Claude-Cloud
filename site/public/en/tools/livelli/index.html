The 5 levels of awareness

Guests are not all at the same point. One is not even thinking of going away. One feels tired but does not know where to go. One is already searching for “hotels to switch off”. One knows you and hesitates. You say something different to each. Getting wrong who you are talking to at that moment is the most expensive copy mistake of all.

The ladder of the 5 levels

From the most ready at the top to the least aware at the bottom.

The levels, from 5 to 1

Your zone this is where demand is born

Don’t try to cover all five.

Focus your copy and content on the two middle levels. That is where new demand is born, the demand that frees you from the OTAs. That is where your best words go.

Where your guest is

These are two different jobs. On Google and AI they find you: be the best answer. On social media you interrupt them: stop them by naming the problem they carry right now, the tiredness, the endless weeks. Not your pool.

Write your two openings

These two are worth more than all the others. They are the levels where you play.
