N°01

Before the channels. The reason to be chosen.

A new website, a new agency, professional photos, AI for the descriptions. All useful. But all of it works on the last step.

Booking brings bookings. It doesn't bring guests of your own.

The guest stays with the platform. You fill your rooms with its customers and pay a commission.

The cause isn't Booking, or the agency. What's missing is a thirty-second sentence that says why to choose your hotel.

Until you have it, no channel can say it for you.

Positioning comes before marketing.

The method in six steps →

A common belief · in the industry

People who run a hotel well work hard and invest where it shows. I did the same, when I was opening restaurants and working sixteen hours a day.

You invest where it shows

  • Renovation
  • Furnishings
  • Technology

You cut where it doesn't

  • Strategy
  • Training
  • Positioning

Working in your hotel and working on your hotel are two different jobs. Positioning is the second one.

N°03

Every ten years the filter changes. This time, faster.

Guests have never chosen from all hotels. They have always chosen from the ones a filter put in front of them.

Yesterday's filter

Travel agencies

A shop window in every town, brochures to leaf through. In ten years Booking took their place.

Today's filter

Booking

A list of a hundred hotels. Guests struggle to tell them apart and often pick the cheapest. But at least your hotel is on the list.

The new filter

AI

Not a list, a choice: one or two hotels. The others, for that guest at that moment, don't appear.

An AI assistant can only describe well what is already clear.

Many hotels already write their descriptions with AI. The result: everyone writes well and everyone says the same things. Writing well is no longer enough.

AI can write the copy for you. It can't decide who you are.

Positioned hotel

  • AI finds it.
  • AI describes it accurately.
  • AI recommends it.
  • The guest arrives already convinced.

Generic hotel

  • AI confuses it with others.
  • AI leaves it out.
  • AI suggests an alternative.
  • Or the cheapest one.

N°05

Silva Splendid. The case, and what it doesn't prove.

Fiuggi. 118 rooms. A 1,600 m² SPA, the largest in Lazio. And marketing that spoke to everyone.

!

A caveat. The project didn't work because of positioning alone. There was also a major renovation.

Positioning on its own doesn't grow revenue. It set the direction, the owners' investment made it credible.

Before

How it presented itself

“4-star hotel with SPA in Fiuggi.”

How the market saw it

  • A dated property.
  • A destination with little appeal.
  • Fiuggi associated with an older crowd.
  • Expensive for the area.

What it was compared with

The other hotels in Fiuggi, at €120-150 a night. On the same list.

After

The new positioning

The Wellness Hotel of Fiuggi.

What it's compared with today

  • No longer the hotels in Fiuggi at €120.
  • The day SPA in Rome at €180 for three hours.
  • The weekend in Tuscany at €600.

Change the comparison and you change the price guests think is fair. That is the positioning choice. The rest is execution.

Frequent questions. The short answers.

What's the difference between the positioning read and the analysis?

The positioning read is free: one page within 3 working days. It tells you whether your hotel is positioned, replaceable or invisible, and where to look for your reason. It contains no ready-made lines.

The analysis costs €697 + VAT: six documents, the reason in one sentence with the evidence, the words to say it and the first 90 days already written. Within 2 days of the questionnaire. Both are delivered in English.

Can I skip the positioning read?

Yes. Write to me at consulting@francescoastolfi.net and we start with the analysis.

Does it work for a three-star too?

The method does, and it’s all in the book. I only do the read and the analysis for 4 and 5-star hotels and resorts.

Is my hotel really generic?

A test: cover the logo on your homepage. If the text only fits you, you're positioned. If it fits a hundred other hotels, not yet.

I've already done a rebrand. Isn't that the same thing?

No. A rebrand decides how the hotel looks. Positioning decides why a guest should choose it.

How much of my time does it take?

The questionnaire, forty minutes. I do the rest: I download and read the reviews myself.

Who puts the analysis into practice?

You, or the people who already work for you. The analysis says what to do and in what words. Execution stays with your team and your suppliers.

Do I need to rebuild my website?

Almost never. You change the headline and the main copy. A new website, if needed, comes later.

Does it work for a group with several hotels?

Yes: each hotel has to stand out from the market and from the others in the group. You start with the analysis of one.

How does the guarantee work?

If within 30 days of delivery the analysis doesn't convince you, I give you all your money back plus another €500 for the time lost. Just write and tell me. I won't ask why.

It covers the work, not the bookings: those depend on what you do next.

And afterwards?

You have six documents and the first three months already written. No subscription, no renewal. You have the plan: if you want me to carry it out, that's separate work, month by month.

For any other question, write to me.
