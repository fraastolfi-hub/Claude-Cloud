How to read a card.

Every label says one precise thing. Even when it says “I don’t know yet”.

Maturity

EstablishedMature
It has existed for a while and has recognisable cases. It works, but someone got there first.
Emerging
There are real cases, still few. The ground is open.
Nascent
Almost nobody does it. Where you read “No iconic case verified yet” there is open space, but no case proves yet that it works.

What this library is not.

  • A menu. You don’t pick the nicest pattern, but the one guests already recognise in your hotel.
  • A ranking. Established does not mean better. It often means crowded.
  • A guarantee. Copying Aman’s pattern does not make your hotel like Aman.
